How to read this

    Our chance is how often we think the player clears (or stays under) the line. Vegas is the same thing according to the odds, with the sportsbook's cut removed. The yellow gap between them is our edge.

    Our numbers come from each player's recent games, his season, and how much the opponent allows to his position. We only list props where we disagree with Vegas by at least 4 points, the player has a steady role, and the gap isn't so large that it probably means injury news we don't have.

    The odds shown are the best price we found and which sportsbook has it. Lines move, so check before you bet.
